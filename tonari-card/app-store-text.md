## Japanese (ja-JP)

### Name

となりカード

### Subtitle

3枚の数字をつなぐ対戦カードパズル

### Promotional Text

1枚置くだけ、でも次の一手が悩ましい。3枚の数字で役を作り、CPU・1台交代プレイ・近くのiPhoneとの対戦を楽しもう。

### Description

「となりカード」は、場に並ぶ3枚の数字カードを入れ替え、役を作って得点する短時間対戦パズルです。

手札から数字を1枚選び、場の左・中央・右のどこかに置くだけ。3枚の並びが役になれば得点です。得点後は最後に置いたカードが中央に残るため、前の一手を受けながら次の展開を考えられます。

得点役:
- ぞろ目: 6点
- 連番: 4点
- はさみ: 3点

先に20点へ到達したプレイヤーの勝利です。先攻の初手は得点になりません。

遊び方:
- CPUと対戦
- 1台のiPhoneを交互に渡して2人対戦
- 近くのiPhoneとローカル通信で対戦

カードを置ける場所や完成する役を光で確認でき、CPUの操作は対戦ログで追えます。短い休憩にも、隣にいる友だちとの一戦にもどうぞ。

※近距離対戦には、Bluetoothまたは同じWi-Fiネットワークとローカルネットワークへのアクセス許可が必要です。
※広告の表示にはインターネット接続が必要です。

## English (en-US)

### Name

Neighbor Numbers

### Subtitle

A quick three-card number duel

### Promotional Text

Play one card and shape the next move. Build patterns from three numbers in CPU, pass-and-play, or nearby iPhone matches.

### Description

Neighbor Numbers is a quick competitive puzzle about replacing one card in a row of three and turning the numbers into scoring patterns.

Choose a number from your hand and play it into the left, center, or right slot. Complete a pattern to score. After scoring, your newly played card stays in the center, so every move becomes the starting point for the next player.

Scoring patterns:
- Triple: 6 points
- Run: 4 points
- Sandwich: 3 points

The first player to reach 20 points wins. The opening player's first turn never scores.

Play modes:
- Challenge the CPU
- Pass one iPhone back and forth for two-player play
- Connect directly to a nearby iPhone for a local match

Clear highlights show playable slots and scoring opportunities, while the move log helps you follow every CPU action. It is easy to learn, quick to play, and surprisingly tactical.

Nearby matches require Bluetooth or the same Wi-Fi network and permission to access the local network. An internet connection is required to display ads.

### What's New

Welcome to Neighbor Numbers. Play against the CPU, share one iPhone, or challenge a nearby player.

## App Review Notes

No account or login is required.

All game modes are available from the first screen. For nearby multiplayer, use two iOS devices with Bluetooth enabled or on the same Wi-Fi network. On one device choose "Create Room" and on the other choose "Find Room," then select the discovered device.

Google AdMob provides a banner on each screen and an interstitial after a completed match. Release builds use production ad unit IDs. The User Messaging Platform consent flow runs before ads are requested where required.

The app uses MultipeerConnectivity for encrypted direct communication between nearby devices. It does not use a developer-operated game server.

Pedalboard Canvas Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled in the Pedalboard Canvas app for iPhone and iPad.

Information Stored by the App

Pedalboard Canvas stores board names and dimensions, pedal names and dimensions, placement data, port data, wiring data, switcher loop settings, and placement artwork created from photos selected by the user. This information is stored on the user's device to provide the app's features and is not transmitted to a server operated by the app developer.

Use of Photos

Photos selected from the user's photo library are used to create pedal artwork for placement planning. The original photos and generated artwork are not transmitted to a server operated by the app developer.

Advertising

Pedalboard Canvas uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. Google may collect information such as approximate location, device information, device identifiers, advertising data, product interactions, crash data, and performance data. For details, review the Google Privacy Policy and Google Mobile Ads SDK data disclosure.

Privacy Choices

Where required, the app uses the Google User Messaging Platform to present privacy choices under European regulations. Users can review or change their choices through Privacy Options in the app when that control is available.

In-App Purchases

Effector Board Pro subscriptions, previous Premium Lifetime purchases, and purchase restoration are processed by Apple through the App Store. The app does not collect or store payment details such as credit card numbers.

Sharing with Third Parties

Except where required by law, Pedalboard Canvas does not provide third parties with board data or images derived from photos stored on the user's device. Advertising-related information may, however, be transmitted to Google by the Google Mobile Ads SDK.

Changes to This Policy

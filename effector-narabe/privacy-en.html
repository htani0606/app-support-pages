Pedalboard Canvas Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled in Pedalboard Canvas for iPhone, iPad, and Mac.

Information Stored by the App

The app stores board names and dimensions, pedal names and dimensions, placement data, port data, wiring data, switcher loop settings, and artwork created from selected photos on your device. Supported versions also store openings, top and underside routes, plug data, and cable-length planning data on the device being used. This information is not transmitted to a server operated by the app developer.

Use of Photos

Photos selected by the user are used to create pedal artwork for placement planning. In the macOS app, a board image may also be used to detect board openings. Original photos, generated artwork, and detection results are not transmitted to a server operated by the app developer.

Advertising

The iPhone and iPad app Pedalboard Canvas uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. Google may collect information such as approximate location, device information, device identifiers, advertising data, product interactions, crash data, and performance data. For details, review the Google Privacy Policy and Google Mobile Ads SDK data disclosure. The macOS app does not use an advertising SDK and does not display ads. Ads are not displayed when a valid Pro, Signal Pro, or previous Premium Lifetime entitlement is active.

Privacy Choices

Where required, the iPhone and iPad app uses the Google User Messaging Platform to present privacy choices under European regulations. Users can review or change their choices through Privacy Options in the app when that control is available. The macOS app does not include advertising-related privacy choices because it does not use an advertising SDK.

In-App Purchases

Pro and Signal Pro subscriptions shared across iPhone, iPad, and Mac, previous Premium Lifetime purchases, and purchase restoration are processed by Apple through the App Store. The app does not collect or store payment details such as credit card numbers.

Sharing with Third Parties

Except where required by law, the app does not provide third parties with board data, routing-planning data, or images derived from photos stored on the user's device. Advertising-related information may, however, be transmitted to Google by the Google Mobile Ads SDK in the iPhone and iPad app. The macOS app does not use an advertising SDK.

Pedalboard Canvas Support

Pedalboard Canvas is an iPhone and iPad app for planning pedal placement and cable routing using the dimensions of your pedalboard and effects pedals.

Contact

For bug reports, questions, or other support requests, contact:

tani@ymail.ne.jp

Main Features

  • Set pedalboard and pedal dimensions in millimeters.
  • Drag registered pedals onto the board to plan their placement.
  • Create placement artwork from a pedal photo.
  • Reorder the signal chain and configure switcher loops and cable guides.
  • Pro adds the real pedal database, advanced port-level routing, junction boxes, power supplies, and accessories.
  • Generate a clean wiring diagram with straight and right-angle routes.
  • Board data and images derived from your photos are stored on your device.

Ads and Effector Board Pro

The free version displays ads and saves one board. Effector Board Pro is a monthly or annual auto-renewable subscription that removes ads and unlocks unlimited boards, the real pedal database, advanced routing, and other Pro tools.

Saved data is not deleted when Pro ends. Boards beyond the free limit and content using Pro-only features become read-only until the subscription is renewed.

Previous Premium Lifetime customers keep ad-free use and a three-board limit. Use Restore Purchases in the app to restore either Pro or a previous lifetime purchase.

Pedalboard Canvas Support

This page supports Pedalboard Canvas for iPhone, iPad, and Mac. Plan pedal placement and routing using real board and pedal dimensions. The features below include those offered in the unified release; availability depends on the installed app version.

Contact

For bug reports, questions, or other support requests, contact:

tani@ymail.ne.jp

Main Features

  • Set pedalboard and pedal dimensions in millimeters.
  • Drag registered pedals onto the board to plan their placement.
  • Create placement artwork from a pedal photo.
  • Reorder the signal chain and configure switcher loops and cable guides.
  • Pro adds the real pedal database, advanced port-level routing, junction boxes, power supplies, and accessories.
  • Generate a clean wiring diagram with straight and right-angle routes.
  • Board data and images derived from your photos are stored on your device.

Signal Pro: Physical Cable-Routing CAD

Signal Pro lets you design physical cable routes on iPhone, iPad, and Mac around real board openings, top and underside surfaces, port positions, and plug clearance. Cable-length estimates are based on the planned route. Signal Pro includes all Pro features. Mac also supports image-assisted opening detection and underside routing PNG export; image detection runs on your Mac.

Boards, photos, openings, ports, routes, and related images are stored on each device and are not sent to a server operated by the app developer. Restoring a purchased plan does not automatically sync board data.

Free, Pro, and Signal Pro

The free plan saves one board. Ads appear in the free iPhone and iPad versions; Mac displays no ads on any plan. Pro includes unlimited boards, the real pedal database, Advanced Chain / Switcher, junction boxes, power supplies, accessories, and board duplication. Signal Pro adds physical cable-routing CAD to all Pro features. Both plans are monthly or annual auto-renewable subscriptions. Use the features included in your purchased plan on supported iPhone, iPad, and Mac versions with the same Apple Account. Pro and Signal Pro also remove ads on iPhone and iPad.

Saved data is not deleted when a subscription ends. Editing of boards beyond the storage limit and paid features is restricted according to your current entitlement. Check the purchase screen for introductory-offer eligibility and pricing. Each customer can use only one introductory offer per subscription group.

Previous Premium Lifetime customers keep ad-free use and a three-board limit. Use Restore Purchases with the same Apple Account to restore Pro, Signal Pro, or a previous lifetime purchase.
